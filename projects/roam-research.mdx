Most note-taking tools present information as a tidy hierarchy — folders inside folders, ideas filed away and forgotten. Roam Research breaks that convention entirely. Its core metaphor is the *graph*: ideas as nodes, relationships as edges, knowledge as a living network that grows more powerful with every connection made. Designing a brand identity for Roam meant internalising that philosophy and translating it into a visual system that feels genuinely native to the product's worldview.

<Info>
  **Year:** 2022 · **Category:** Brand Identity
</Info>

## The Design Challenge

Roam occupies a distinctive niche — it is a tool beloved by researchers, writers, and systems thinkers who value depth over convenience. The community is intellectually serious and aesthetically discerning. Any brand work needed to honour that without becoming inaccessible or cold. The challenge was to create a visual language that felt rigorous and considered, but also alive — something that communicated the joy of unexpected connection, the pleasure of following a thread of thought wherever it leads.

## Design Approach

The identity draws on the structural logic of the product itself. Where traditional tools suggest linearity and containment, the visual language here embraces *emergence* — forms that appear to grow and link rather than stack and sort. Typography is treated with care, reflecting the tool's roots in serious writing and long-form thought. The colour palette is restrained, giving the visual system a focused, distraction-free quality that mirrors what Roam tries to provide for its users.

The mark itself needed to function at small scale — as a favicon, app icon, and interface element — while also carrying enough character to anchor editorial and marketing contexts. The solution lives at the intersection of geometric precision and organic connectivity, a nod to the graph database architecture that makes Roam's non-linear thinking possible.

## Visual Identity Decisions

The identity system was built to be flexible without being arbitrary. Each element — logotype, icon, typographic choices, spatial relationships — reflects the same underlying idea: that meaning emerges from connection, not from isolation. The result is a brand that feels coherent across contexts precisely because it is rooted in a single, clear conceptual principle rather than a surface-level aesthetic trend.

## Outcome

The identity establishes Roam Research as a product with intellectual conviction. It signals to the tool's core audience — people who take thinking seriously — that this is software built by people who think the same way. For potential partners, investors, or new users encountering the brand for the first time, it communicates craft, depth, and a clear point of view.
