Education has a design problem. So much of what surrounds academic subjects — particularly the sciences and mathematics — communicates through visual language that is either aridly institutional or naively cartoonish. Maths Physics Bay is working against that grain: building a presence that makes these subjects feel genuinely compelling, intellectually exciting, and approachable without ever talking down. The identity work documented here is part of that ongoing effort.

<Note>
  **Year:** 2023 · **Category:** Brand Identity · **Status:** Work in Progress — this project is actively evolving.
</Note>

## The Context

Maths Physics Bay operates in the space between formal education and independent learning — a space that has grown significantly, and that rewards brands capable of building genuine trust and intellectual credibility with their audience. The visual identity needed to reflect the precision and elegance inherent in mathematics and physics, while feeling warm and human enough to invite people who might not yet see themselves as "science people."

## Design Approach

The core tension in this project is between *rigour* and *accessibility* — two qualities that are sometimes assumed to be in conflict, but which the best educational design holds together. The visual system draws on the formal vocabulary of scientific notation, diagrams, and typographic precision, reinterpreting these familiar references through a contemporary, considered lens.

There is something beautiful about the way mathematical and physical systems describe the world — the elegance of a well-formed equation, the clarity of a well-constructed diagram. The identity tries to make that beauty visible and felt, rather than relegating it to the background of content delivery.

## Visual Identity Decisions

The work in progress reflects an iterative process — testing how different marks, typographic choices, and colour decisions perform across the contexts Maths Physics Bay inhabits. Particular attention has been paid to how the identity scales: from small digital UI contexts through to larger format educational materials. The system needs to work hard in digital environments, where the audience most commonly encounters it.

Colour plays a significant role in the developing identity, helping to differentiate subject areas and content types while maintaining overall coherence. The palette avoids the default blues and greens of the broader edtech sector, carving out more distinctive visual territory.

## Where the Work is Headed

As an evolving project, Maths Physics Bay continues to be refined in response to how the brand grows in use. The strongest brand identities are not designed once and frozen — they are developed through application, feedback, and an ongoing conversation between the identity system and the people it serves. This project is a live example of that process.
