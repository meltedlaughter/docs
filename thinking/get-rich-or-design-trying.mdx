Design is a craft. It's also a career, a negotiation, a leap of faith, and occasionally a comedy of errors. *Get Rich or Design Trying* is an honest conversation about all of it — the work, the business behind the work, and the strange, rewarding path of building a life around making things.

The podcast sits at the intersection of design practice and creative ambition. Not the polished, retrospective version of success you see in award showcases — but the real, in-progress experience of figuring out how to do meaningful work, get paid for it, and keep growing as a designer and a human being.

---

## What We Talk About

Each episode pulls at a different thread of the design life:

**The business of design** — Pricing, positioning, client relationships, the gap between design school and design practice. How do you talk about value? How do you build a sustainable studio or freelance career without losing your creative soul in the spreadsheet?

**Creative career development** — How designers grow, pivot, specialise, and reinvent themselves. The messy, non-linear reality of building a body of work over time, across disciplines, and through the inevitable dry spells.

**Making things that matter** — What does it mean for work to be meaningful? How do you hold onto that ambition in commercial contexts? These conversations explore the tension between craft and compromise, and where the genuinely interesting work tends to live.

**Process and practice** — The habits, methods, rituals, and tools that shape how designers actually work day to day. Not the theoretical ideal, but the real routine.

---

## Why "Get Rich or Design Trying"?

The title is partly a joke — and partly not. Design is one of those fields where people are routinely asked to work for exposure, to under-price themselves, to treat financial sustainability as somehow at odds with creative integrity. The podcast refuses that false choice.

You can care deeply about the quality and impact of what you make *and* build a livelihood that supports that ambition. That's the conversation. That's the work.
