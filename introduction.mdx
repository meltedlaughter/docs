Kevin Glynn is an interaction and product designer based in Ireland, working at the intersection of physical products, digital experiences, and human behaviour. His practice spans consumer hardware, speculative futures, brand systems, and service design — always anchored in the belief that good design makes the invisible legible: the systems we use, the habits we form, and the choices we make every day.

## Approach & Philosophy

Kevin designs for behaviour change — working with the grain of how people actually live, move, and decide rather than against it. His process combines rigorous research with hands-on prototyping, bridging the gap between industrial craft and interaction design. Transparency is a recurring commitment: systems and products should reveal their logic to the people who use them, not obscure it.

Influences include Marshall McLuhan's ideas on media as extensions of the body, Anthony Dunne's speculative practice, Bret Victor's demand for immediate feedback in design tools, and Wilson Miner's writing on the long-term effects of designed environments on human behaviour.
