There is something fundamentally social about craft beer — it is a culture built on shared discovery, on passing a glass across the table and saying *try this*. Dublin Beer Tasting exists to formalise and celebrate that ritual, bringing together enthusiasts, brewers, and curious newcomers around the shared pleasure of tasting well-made beer. The brand identity needed to carry that warmth and conviviality while also projecting enough credibility to stand alongside the serious craft producers it champions.

</Info>

## The Brief

Dublin Beer Tasting occupies an interesting position: it is simultaneously a community platform and a curatorial voice. It needs to feel approachable and inviting — not gatekeeping, not intimidating — while also signalling genuine expertise and taste. The identity had to work across multiple contexts: social media, event materials, printed ephemera, merchandise, and digital environments. Consistency without rigidity was the guiding principle.

## Design Approach

The visual language leans into the tactile, material qualities of craft brewing culture. There is a richness and warmth to the palette, echoing the amber hues of lager, the deep mahogany of stout, the golden glow of a well-poured session ale. But the system avoids the clichés of Irish pub nostalgia — no shamrocks, no faux-vintage distressing. Instead, it finds a contemporary vernacular that feels rooted in Dublin's present-tense creative energy.

Typography is used confidently, with a hierarchy that communicates both personality and clarity. The logotype is distinctive enough to anchor the brand, while the supporting type system is flexible enough to handle everything from Instagram captions to event programmes.

## Visual Identity Decisions

The identity was developed with social media as a primary canvas — Dublin Beer Tasting's Instagram presence is central to how the community experiences the brand day to day. This shaped decisions around contrast, legibility at small sizes, and the construction of visual templates that individual posts could inhabit without the system feeling either chaotic or robotically uniform.

The mark needed to feel right whether it appeared on a printed coaster, an Instagram story, or a tote bag. That range of application demanded a design that was simultaneously bold and economical — doing the most with the fewest elements.

## Outcome

The Dublin Beer Tasting identity succeeds in making craft beer culture feel genuinely accessible. It is a brand you want to engage with — one that invites you in rather than performing exclusivity. For the client, it provided a consistent visual foundation from which to grow: a recognisable presence that travels well across every touchpoint the community encounters.
