## Send a Message

Prefer to write first? The contact form is the right place. Here's what to include:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your name">
    What do you go by? First name, full name, preferred handle — whatever you'd like to be addressed as.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your email address">
    Where should a reply land? A straightforward email address is all that's needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="LinkedIn (optional)">
    If you'd like to connect professionally or share a bit of context about your background, a LinkedIn profile link is welcome — but entirely optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your message">
    What's on your mind? A project brief, a question, a podcast topic, a collaboration idea — bring it. The more context you share, the more useful a reply will be.
  </Step>
</Steps>
